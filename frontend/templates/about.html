About Us

Guided by our steadfast commitment to justice, PIN is dedicated to timely and thoughtful action: initiating and supporting efforts that advance our shared cause. PIN exists to work in solidarity for a Free Palestine, envisioning the abolition of the apartheid system

Through PIN, we encourage and support each other as we work toward the liberation of Palestine. We collaborate with and support the work of other organizations and individuals in our community, serving as both a bridge and a unifying force for interconnection.
