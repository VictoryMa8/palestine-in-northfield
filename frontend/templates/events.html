Filmlab Palestine's Palestine Cinema Days

November 1st through the 3rd

HOSTED BY: St. Olaf College - Students for Justice in Palestine

MORE INFO: Instagram Page

On November 2nd, the anniversary of the Balfour Declaration, Palestine Cinema Days organizes over 500 Palestinian film screenings around the world. This global effort is a show of solidarity with Palestine, an act of amplification for censored Palestinian voices, and a contribution to shifting the distorted narrative. SJP at St. Olaf College is hosting screenings for four films. More information on the posters below!

CHALK THEIR NAMES: Children Killed in Gaza

LAST CHALKING: October 29th, 7:30 AM at Bridge Square in Northfield

SPONSORED BY: Palestine in Northfield

MORE INFO: palestineinnorthfield@gmail.com

Every Wednesday, Northfielders gather to chalk the names of children killed in Gaza. Since October 7, 2023, Israeli attacks have killed more than 18,500 children in Gaza. Thousands more are missing under the rubble.

Campaign to Repeal Minnesota's Anti-Boycott Laws

SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 13: 10:00 AM via Zoom

SPONSORED BY: Middle East Peace Now

PRESENTERS: Mohammed Mayaleh and Angel Dobrow

MORE INFO & REGISTRATION: Click Here

In 2017, Minnesota joined 36 other states by introducing into law measures that require businesses and individuals who want to obtain a contract over $50,000 with the state of Minnesota to certify they do not and will not boycott the state of Israel. These anti-boycott laws directly violate our constitutionally protected right to free speech. These laws were formulated by ALEC (American Legislative Exchange Council), which crafts legislation to implement conservative and repressive goals.

Bells of Solidarity: A Wordless Prayer for Humanity in Gaza

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 16: 4:00 PM at Bridge Square in Northfield

SPONSORED BY: Palestine in Northfield & Northfielders for Justice in Palestine/Israel

MORE INFO: palestineinnorthfield@gmail.com, Northfield News Article

Churches in Northfield will simultaneously ring bells on September 16th as a Wordless Prayer for Humanity in Gaza. WHO PROFITS FROM THE WAR IN GAZA: A Reading from the UN Report

SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 28: 2:00 PM at the Public Library in Northfield

SPONSORED BY: Northfielders for Justice in Palestine/Israel

MORE INFO: info.njpmn@gmail.com

“Show me who makes a profit from war, and I'll show you how to stop the war” -Henry Ford

Who is profiting from the war in Gaza?

To help answer that question, NJP/I invites you attend a presentation of the UN report by Francesca Albenese, "From Economy of Occupation to Economy of Genocide." on Sunday September 28, 2-4 p.m., in the Northfield Public Library. Francesca Albanese (b. 1977) is an Italian legal scholar and expert on human rights. In May 2022 she was appointed the United Nations Special Rapporteur on the Occupied Palestinian Territories.
